# Security Policy

## Responsible disclosure

Report potential vulnerabilities privately to the maintainers through the repository security advisory workflow or designated security contact channels.

Please include:
- affected component/path
- impact summary
- reproduction details
- suggested mitigation (if available)

Do not publicly disclose exploitable details before coordinated remediation.

## Policy integrity and abuse response

This repository defines policy decision logic and interfaces. Integrity of policy artifacts, reason codes, and versioning is security-sensitive. Suspected abuse-pattern evasion and policy bypass attempts should be escalated through incident response.

## Incident severity placeholders

- Severity 1 (Critical): active exploit with material protocol risk
- Severity 2 (High): likely exploit path with strong impact potential
- Severity 3 (Medium): constrained exploitability or mitigated impact
- Severity 4 (Low): hard-to-exploit or limited impact findings
